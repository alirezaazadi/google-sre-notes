how to study

A useful reading loop

Read the explanation
→
Redraw the diagram
→
Redo the math
→
Explain the failure mode
→
Answer aloud
Useful rule: if you can explain why a design or operational practice works, what breaks without it, and what trade-off it introduces, you understand the topic much better than if you only remember its definition.
